**FT412 (LOW) — a production seam that does I/O per item has a test that pins how its cost grows.**

A seam that syncs, locks, renames, or starts a process for each item can grow its cost
without notice. A test that counts the seam's operations at 1 item and at N items, and that
asserts the designed growth, turns that regression red.
`TestRecordSavesIndependentOfEntryCount` is the first such test.

The shape finds which seams need such a test, and whether `craft-tdd` states the pattern.
The reviewer proposed Big-O notes on tests and then chose this executable form, because a
comment cannot be checked.
