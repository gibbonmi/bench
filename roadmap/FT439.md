**FT439 (MEDIUM) — a comment-only correction after a chunk freeze keeps its verifications and its review chain.**

Three sources disagree on a comment-only correction after a chunk freeze. The bounded
repair policy treats it as evidence-only but still requires a refreeze. The covered-digest
check accepts a proved comment gap. The review-chain check requires exact digest equality
or a fold, so a comment gap breaks the chain. A refreeze then orphans every recorded
verification of the chunk, and the chain gap forces a new review round.

The reviewer decided on 2026-10-21 to extend the proved comment gap. The review-chain check
accepts a proved comment gap, as the covered-digest check does, and the bounded repair
policy takes no refreeze for it. This decision supersedes the 2026-10-18 rule for a
comment-only correction.
