**FT439 (MEDIUM) — a comment-only correction after a chunk freeze keeps its verifications and its review chain.**

Two sources still disagree on a comment-only correction after a chunk freeze. The
covered-digest check accepts a proved comment gap. The review-chain check compares the next
chunk base with the previous chunk digest, or with a covering fold digest, by equality. A
comment gap therefore breaks the chain and forces a new review round. The bounded repair
policy still requires a refreeze for a comment-only correction before the green checkpoint.
Since FT445, each verification entry binds to its own commit, so a refreeze keeps the
recorded verifications of the chunk.

The reviewer decided on 2026-10-21 to extend the proved comment gap. The review-chain check
accepts a proved comment gap, as the covered-digest check does, and the bounded repair
policy takes no refreeze for it. This decision supersedes the 2026-10-18 rule for a
comment-only correction.
