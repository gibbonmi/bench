**FT391 (HIGH, decision required) — a hotfix lane lands a small change at once, outside the roadmap row and commitment ceremony.**

The roadmap row and the commitment guardrails queue larger fixes and features. They also
apply to a small change today. A small change is a light-path idea or learning at drain
time, or a one-file change such as a new ADR. That change pays a row, a commitment
admission, a binding plan, and a delivery landing.

Shape the lane. The open decisions are these:

- the size and kind of change that qualifies, and which observable decides it;
- whether bookkeeping, such as a spec retirement after its verified delivery or an ADR
  write, is a planning path that needs no delivery binding;
- how the lane obeys the commitment rule, or which exception to that rule the reviewer
  adopts;
- the evidence that a hotfix landing leaves in place of a delivery fact.

The lane changes the delivery rules in the shared Bench guide, so the result is a kit edit
under `craft-synthesis`.
