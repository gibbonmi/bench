**FT244 (LOW) — a standard scratch directory for worktree runs.**

`bench worktree create` provisions one per-assignment scratch directory,
`bench worktree exec` exposes it (for example `BENCH_SCRATCH`), and
`bench worktree release` removes it with the assignment. Delegate charges,
review outputs, patch copies, and probe copies live beside the worktree instead
of the harness temp directory.
