**FT325 (LOW, decision required) — a canary fixture does not depend on the physical wrap of guidance prose.**

An anchor normalizes whitespace, but a canary fixture quotes physical line bytes. A
harmless reflow or a test move then turns the gate red or stops a fixture bite. A
guidance-prose formatter with one canonical wrap has value only after the fixtures stop
quoting the wrap.

Decide whether the fixtures stop quoting physical bytes. The formatter follows that
decision and is not specified before it.
