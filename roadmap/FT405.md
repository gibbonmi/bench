**FT405 (MEDIUM, decision required) — one `bench record ticket` verb lets a ticket author amend, freeze, and record its chunk in one call.**

A ticket author commits its ticket, and the orchestrator then freezes the chunk and
resumes the author to record its verification entries. That round trip costs one
exchange for each ticket, and the record order is easy to get wrong.

`bench record ticket <slug> --chunk <id> --excerpts <dir>` records the plan amendment
when the digest moved, and it freezes the chunk at `HEAD`. It then records one
verification entry for each planned requirement from a folder of excerpts named by
requirement id. The
implement-spec guidance then gives the freeze and the verification records to the ticket
author.

The reviewer decides whether the guidance moves the freeze to the author, because the
orchestrator owns the freeze today. The verb composes the shipped `bench record
assignment` and `bench record verification` forms.

An excerpt must exist as a file today, and an unspilled response leaves no file. The
verb or the verification form can read an excerpt from the last response of the
worktree, so an author copies no excerpt by hand.
