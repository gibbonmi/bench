**FT401 (LOW, decision required) — the refusals outside the write verbs print their routes from the refusal-route registry.**

Each write-verb refusal routes through one registry face with a declared agent or reviewer
authority. A static check refuses a route literal outside the registry, and
`bench recovery` renders the matrix from the registry. Two refusal families stay outside:

- The gate run-state refusals outside the checkpoint funnel print through `operational`
  with a reason line only.
- The non-write worktree verbs `list`, `path`, `clean`, `release`, and `build` keep
  `recoveryRoute` and have no registry face.

The `--in` tree-target refusal also prints outside the registry, and FT341 owns that
surface. Two more sites compose route text outside the registry: the sibling route of
`bench worktree create --from`, and the `internal/adopt` compatibility guidance. A route
that a non-write verb prints also needs a follow fixture that reaches its cleared cause.
The reviewer decides whether these families join the registry.
