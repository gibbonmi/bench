**FT223 (LOW, decision required) — `bench commit`'s inherited-verdict refusal misreads as a red gate.**

`internal/landing/landing.go` refuses prospective authorization
whenever the composed tree's verdict is `Kind != authorization.Green`,
including an inherited (partial-evidence) verdict that is not a failure.
`error: prospective authorization refused: inherited` names an internal verdict
kind, not an operator action. A caller-printed `gate: red` alongside it
misreports a partial verdict as a failing one, costing a fresh gate run to
disprove. The reviewer decides between two fixes. Reword the refusal in
operator terms naming `bench gate --fresh` as the next action and stop
emitting `gate: red` for it, or have `bench commit` escalate to a fresh
prospective gate itself instead of refusing.
