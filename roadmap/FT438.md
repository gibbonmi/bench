**FT438 (HIGH) — a delegated repair's assignment and plan records keep a test-only repair eligible for `bench record confirmation`.**

A test-only repair can close through `bench record confirmation` with no confirming
review round. The confirmation proves that the repair delta changed only tests, over a
digest that covers the spec bytes. A delegated repair always commits an assignment record
to the completion plan, so its delta holds a plan file, and the confirmation refuses it.
The test-only route therefore cannot serve any delegated repair, and each such repair
pays a full three-axis confirming round.

The confirmation leaves the assignment block of the completion plan out of the test-only
proof. The reviewer decided on 2026-10-21 that the exemption covers the assignment block
only. A change to the plan fence, a `Writes:` widening, the review record, or the seam cell
of an added coverage row still takes a confirming round.
