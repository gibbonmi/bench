**FT174 (MEDIUM) — ticket files have one enforced dependency, ownership, and mutation grammar.**

There is no ticket parser in the tree. The grammar this row assumes — blocked-by
edges, ownership fences, Red-mutation rows, duplicate-ID rejection — is
unimplemented. The symbol the row leans on exists only inside unlanded ticket
prose. What does parse today is spec ownership fences, in preflight.

The light-path close step now ships (FT229): a ticket is consumed on landing, and
a tickets-only folder no longer survives as residue with no owner. So the grammar
can be designed without carrying orphaned-ticket disposition as an open question.
What remains is the parser itself, and an advisory fence that must include the
established dispatcher, renderer, and terminal-lifecycle owners. Recent
builds had to name them by hand.

When a ticket moves or reflows an anchor, its `Writes:` list names every canary
fixture that pins that line. The parser can then reject a fence that leaves the
red-capable fixture outside the ticket.
