**FT400 (MEDIUM, decision required) — a ticket author near 300k context tokens finishes its ticket, and a fresh recorded session takes later work.**

The reviewer made a standing rule during the FT393 build. A delegate near 300k tokens
finishes its current ticket, and a fresh recorded session takes its later work. A
verification-only transfer is the cheap form of the rule. No guidance states the rule, and
no transfer trigger names a context reason.

`craft-line` states the rule in its continuation section, and the `craft-delegate`
transfer list names it. The slicing face is closed: on 2026-10-23 the reviewer set the
slicing size to about 150k tokens of author context, and `craft-tickets` now states it.
The reviewer also decides whether that rule states a bytes-per-token ratio for the
estimate from the `Writes:` byte total. The reviewer decides whether a transfer records a new trigger token or
`user-directed`. The reviewer also decides whether two blocked returns from one author
trigger a transfer.

FT457 ships the fresh narrow continuation rule that the reviewer directed on 2026-10-26.
The estimate model is still open. The FT314 and FT450 builds point to an estimate of the
`Writes:` bytes, the read set outside `Writes:`, about 5k tokens per witness probe, and one
lane retry. That model reverses the `craft-tickets` clause that sizes a ticket not from its
expected reads.
