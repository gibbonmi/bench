**FT400 (MEDIUM, decision required) — a ticket author near 300k context tokens finishes its ticket, and a fresh recorded session takes later work.**

The reviewer made a standing rule during the FT393 build. A delegate near 300k tokens
finishes its current ticket, and a fresh recorded session takes its later work. A
verification-only transfer is the cheap form of the rule. No guidance states the rule, and
no transfer trigger names a context reason.

`craft-line` states the rule in its continuation section, and the `craft-delegate`
transfer list names it. The slicing guidance sizes each ticket so that its author finishes
under 300k tokens. The reviewer decides whether a transfer records a new trigger token or
`user-directed`. The reviewer also decides whether two blocked returns from one author
trigger a transfer.
