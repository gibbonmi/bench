**FT419 (MEDIUM) — a build that adds a persisted ledger or review-record field writes and lands through a binary that reads that field.**

A kit build can add a field to a persisted record, such as the intent ledger or a review
record. The installed binary decodes those records strictly, so it refuses the new field.
A checkpoint in the build worktree can then write a record that the `main` binary refuses.
The landing gate subject also cannot read the record that the build wrote.

The strict decoder stays. On 2026-10-13 the reviewer chose the candidate binary: the
checkpoints and the landing of a kit build run through the build's own candidate binary.
The spec of a build that adds such a field names the pre-landing writer and the landing
binary in its edge inventory.
