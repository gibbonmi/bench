**FT322 (LOW) — `craft-line` resolves a recorded fork line and times a consultation question.**

A fork inherits the model of the session that starts it. A recorded line can name a
different model, and the skill does not say which one wins. The skill also does not say
when an open judgment call becomes a consultation question. One kit edit adds these rules:

- When the coordinator model differs from the recorded line, a recorded fork becomes a fresh writer on the recorded model.
- The coordinator flags that change of venue for reviewer veto.
- A judgment call that the gate cannot settle becomes a read-only consultation question when it appears.

FT222 owns the tier routing policy. This row owns only the fork resolution.
