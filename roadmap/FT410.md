**FT410 (LOW) — the last chunk of a build closes through the complete checkpoint alone.**

The complete checkpoint grades every chunk's verification and review entries again. A
chunk checkpoint gate before it therefore repeats the same whole-gate run. The implement
phase runs a chunk checkpoint gate only before a successor chunk, and the final chunk goes
straight to final reconciliation.
