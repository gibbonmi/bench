**FT169 (MEDIUM) — the landing command's refusals name what to do next, and its authority is decided.**

`bench worktree land` ships. What remains is its refusal surface and the
authority questions nobody has answered.

A refusal reading "reviewed source range or ownership fence is invalid" names no
offending path, though preflight review already computes it. An incomplete
post-publication release must name the ignored residue and print the exact
continuation. Landing recovery from a request token that does not survive a
session has no path at all.

The undecided half is the reviewer's. It covers this command's exact authority,
and how an interrupted landing recovers. It also covers how it relates to
`bench commit` and `bench worktree release`, and whether the hook pool's
one-active limit belongs here.

The refusal half shipped on 2026-08-23. The landing accepts abbreviated ids and
rebuilds its own binary. It composes onto a moved destination by taking the
source's handoff and the destination's capture files. It treats `capture/` as
always-authorized for the landing range, and it reports every refusal in one
preflight, with the offending paths named. The authority half stays open; the
parked spec-less-landing and phase-owned-merge-rules ideas carry its next shape.
