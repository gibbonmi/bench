**FT169 (MEDIUM) — the landing command names its refusals and recovery, and its authority is decided.**

`bench worktree land` needs refusal messages that identify the failed path,
the recovery state, and the next command. The messages must distinguish a
completed conflict resolution from a merge that still needs `git merge --continue`.

The command must name an incomplete publication's ignored residue and its
continuation. It must also recover a landing when a request token does not
survive the session. A stale executable and an undeclared ignored residue need
an exact remedy.

The reviewer decides this command's authority, its relation to `bench commit`
and `bench worktree release`, and the hook pool's one-active limit. The decision
also covers board conflicts, spec staging, the primary-checkout landing route,
and one parse-seam normalization for `--spec` slug and path forms.

FT249 owns the capture-storage decision. FT169 only requires the landing to
state its capture-related refusal and recovery route after that decision.
