**FT232 (EXPERIMENT, decision required) — repair-loop tripwire: an advisory signal from gate records.**
Nothing detects that an agent is patching in a loop; the mechanism that fixes it
is reviewer-invoked, so the trigger depends on a human noticing. An advisory row
derived from the gate's own records would surface the loop at the moment it
forms. Experiment, not a committed build: the row exists to hold the design and
its evidence question — whether the records already carry enough signal to
distinguish a repair loop from ordinary iteration.
