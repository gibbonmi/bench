**FT452 (MEDIUM) — the planned-cell refusal of `bench record ticket` reads only the ticket's own `Covers:` rows.**

`bench record ticket` refuses when any coverage row of the chunk still holds a planned cell
at the freeze tip. In a multi-ticket chunk, a non-last ticket therefore cannot record until
the last ticket cites its rows.

The reviewer decided on 2026-10-25 to narrow the refusal. The refusal reads only the rows
that the recording ticket covers, so a non-last ticket records at its own commit.
