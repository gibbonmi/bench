**FT408 (MEDIUM) — an assignment with no landed commit and uncommitted output is not landed.**

`bench worktree list` marks an assignment `landed=true` when its tip is a `main` commit. It
does so even when the assignment has no commit of its own and holds uncommitted output. The
drain orphan step and the session-start landed count then treat that work as landed.

A landed verdict needs at least one assignment commit that `main` contains. An assignment
with no commit and dirty output is unlanded work. The reviewer decides the salvage of each
such worktree.
