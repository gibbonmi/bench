**FT415 (LOW) — a red `bench test` lists every failed want line of a multi-rule harness.**

A red `bench test` shows the first failure line of a harness that breaks many rules.
Authors then rerun with `--full` and read a nested spill to see each broken rule. The default
projection lists each failed want line. It also prints the total wall time of the run.

A red `bench test` also keeps each line of a multi-line `t.Fatalf` message.
