**FT463 (HIGH) — a spec-backed build pays for its checks and its change work, and each retro measures what the build cost.**

The FT451 build took 16.1 hours of wall time for 16 tickets. Its writers spent 37% of
their time on orientation, 26% on record steps, and 37% on change work. The recorded
author requirement runs took 95.5 minutes, which is the largest record item. Each writer
paid 24.5 fixed calls and 6.4 minutes for record steps. Of 145 commits, 108 were plan,
review, record, or spec commits.

The retro has no cost field, and the drain has no route that removes a step. Thus each
build can add a step, and no build measures the steps that it pays for.

The row makes these cuts:

- The retro scaffold derives a cost section from git and the trace store. A budget
  overrun makes the drain propose one removal or one merge, and it never makes the gate red.
- An author records no requirement runs. The witness reds, the `--mutants` probe, the lane
  pass, and the chunk checkpoint grade stay.
- One record verb after the ticket commit replaces the separate record steps, and it holds
  the dispatch facts.
- A dispatch or a widen inside signed-off scope takes no plan commit. The plan digest covers
  only the approved plan, the charge binds the ticket base, and a widen writes no learning.
- The success output of `bench commit` and of `bench probe --mutants` fits in 10 lines.
- If the cost is low, the chunk grade starts at the freeze, beside the review axes.

The build keeps every gate, every lane pass, each chunk checkpoint grade, the witness reds,
and the probes. It also keeps both reviews, each done-claim check, a fresh author per
ticket, a fresh repair session, and each reviewer stop.

The reviewer decided on 2026-10-28 that this row goes before FT402-git-cache. The reviewer
also decided that a slicing target needs data. Thus the spec measures the graph depth and
the serial checkpoints of recent builds before it sets any slicing target.

The spec settles these open decisions:

- whether the checkpoint grade is the only green evidence for a ticket check
- the name and the grammar of the one record verb
- whether a read-only exec child gets a larger output bound than a Bench verb
- whether a widen inside a signed-off directory needs a plan commit
- which record owns the cost evidence, and which budget numbers the drain uses
- whether the removal of a duplicated step waits for FT231
