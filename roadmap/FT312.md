**FT312 (MEDIUM) — a native `bench` verb codifies the cross-harness reviewer invocation.**

A native `bench` verb could replace the hand-typed `claude -p` and `codex
exec` invocations `references/cross-harness-reviewers.md` currently
documents as recipes. Every cross-harness falsification or review pass
re-derives the same long flag string, including the heredoc-for-stdin-close
workaround and the `-o` output-file convention.

The reviewer decides the verb's shape: one verb with a harness flag, or one
verb per harness. `bench-craft-cli`'s AXI conventions govern its output
schema and error grammar.
