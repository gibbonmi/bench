**FT342 (HIGH, decision required) — a chunked build has one sanctioned route for a moved `main`, from preflight through completion and landing.**

When `main` moves during a chunked build, four surfaces disagree. Bare `bench preflight
build` grades `base-current` red and names `bench worktree merge --from main`. The review
chain refuses a `main` merge after the first chunk. The completion gate refuses a composed
tree that differs from the reviewed source, so the landing needs a `main` merge after the
last chunk. The review preflight then refuses a charge for that merge, because the `main`
paths are outside every ownership fence.

The decision names one route for a moved `main`, for example a destination fold after the
last chunk. The build preflight, the review preflight, the completion check, and the phase
guidance read one owner of that route. After the fold, the final checks include the package
of each merged test file. The review phase then states the route, its review round, and its
final checks. A change to `base-current` is part of this decision.
