**FT318 (MEDIUM) — a `bench record` refusal names its repair form, and the completion form records a capability-blocked row's decided value.**

`bench record` writes the chunk, verification, review, amendment, and completion entries
of the review record. It computes each digest and parses the whole record before it
writes. `bench record completion` reconciles every planned row as `covered`.

Three faces remain:

- The completion form records the decided value of a capability-blocked row.
- `bench record chunk` refuses when the record plan digest differs from the plan at the
  tip. The refusal names `bench record amendment`, so the amendment comes before the
  chunk entry at each freeze.
- Each checkpoint refusal of a record entry names the `bench record` form that repairs it.

The capability-blocked value waits on the FT317 decision.
