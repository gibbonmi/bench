**FT448 (MEDIUM) — a repair record reruns the requirements that its delta reaches and carries the rest forward.**

A repair session reruns every plan requirement of its ticket at the repair commit. This
includes the whole system suite, about 5 minutes, for a doc fix or a test-only pin. The
record verb refuses a repair record that lacks an owed requirement.

The record verb accepts a repair entry that carries forward each requirement whose packages
the repair delta cannot reach. The changed-package selector of the delta checkpoint decides
the reach, and the repair reruns the rest.
