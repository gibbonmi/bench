**FT314 (MEDIUM) — an unchanged landing composition reuses gate evidence only under a complete authorization key.**

The landing gate already reuses evidence during a retry when the graded tree
and baseline runner stay unchanged. A cross-run reuse would skip a duplicate
full gate for an unchanged composition.

The reviewer approved that reuse on 2026-10-14. The authorization key binds
the composed tree, gate version, toolchain, and capability posture, and it
covers a landing that reuses its `bench worktree merge` verdict. A change to
any key member runs the full gate.

The design must preserve the gate as the oracle. It must not treat an old
receipt as authority for a different execution environment.

The same design decides how a review-venue merge discloses its full-gate cost before execution.
The projection must preserve the gate schedule.

A `bench worktree merge --from main` and the landing that follows grade the same composition
with two full gates. The same key decides whether the landing reuses the merge verdict. A
drain that lands several light-path sources could also land them in order under one gate.
