**FT484 (MEDIUM, decision required) — `bench test --in` and `bench probe --in` rebuild a stale worktree build once, as `bench commit` does.**

A worktree `bench commit` rebuilds the worktree build when its seal does not verify. The
`--in` route of `bench test` and `bench probe` refuses a stale build. Ticket authors
therefore rebuild the worktree by hand after each Go edit. The decided FT341 contract says
that a missing or stale build refuses, so this row reverses a closed decision. FT341 is a
committed outcome, so a change reaches it only through `bench commitment plan`.

The reviewer decides whether `--in` rebuilds once or keeps the refusal.
