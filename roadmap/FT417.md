**FT417 (LOW) — a reworded spec sentence keeps each predicate of the old sentence unless a decision deletes it.**

A spec author who rewords an anchored lead can drop a rule clause. The FT100 spec fork
reworded the B40 lead of decision #4 and dropped its timing rule. The fork read the
reword permission of decision #10 as a replacement. The spec review caught it, and the
round took two iterations.

`craft-spec` gains one authoring rule. A reworded sentence keeps each predicate of the old
sentence. New content joins as new sentences, unless the decision source deletes a clause.
The spec review axis grades a reword against that rule.
