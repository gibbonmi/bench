**FT390 (HIGH) — a commitment deliverable either closes its outcome or refuses, and a commitment plan is canonical and survives a settled deliverable.**

The commitment plan and the delivery record share one owner surface. Three faces remain:

- A plan that binds a deliverable to an outcome with sources lists the obligations that
  the deliverable closes. Today the plan accepts an empty obligation list. The completion
  landing then records no delivery fact, because `Deliver` returns the policy unchanged.
  Either the plan refuses that binding, or the completion landing refuses a completion
  that records no fact.
- A plan that settles or removes a deliverable does not need that deliverable on `main`.
  Today `BuildPlan` adds each unsettled source of the current policy, and the source
  check reads each one at `main`. A deleted deliverable folder then blocks every plan.
- The plan identity sorts its policy sources in one canonical order. Today it hashes them
  in traversal order, so a change to that order can change the identity while every bound
  fact stays the same.
