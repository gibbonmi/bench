**FT319 (MEDIUM) — the implement and review phase files state the chunk chain and the record order.**

`/bench-implement-spec` and `/bench-review-implementation` leave the chunk chain partly
unstated. The build paid for each gap with a refused checkpoint or an added review round.
One kit edit adds these rules:

- A chunk base is the close commit of the predecessor chunk, never a later plan commit.
- Plan commits and `main` merges land before the ticket merge, and only record commits come after the chunk tip.
- The reconciliation commit joins the review delta of the last chunk.
- The author commits the verification and probe record before the axis dispatch.
- A chunk that ends on a repair gets one confirming round of all three axes at its final tip.
- A review worktree moves to the record commit, and the frozen pair still names the source tip.
- The build preflight runs after each ticket commit, through the worktree.
- The assessment record is written before the landing step.
- On one shared tree, only one axis runs tests or probes while the other axes read. A probing axis otherwise takes its own worktree.

The reviewer sets one number in the same edit: the cap on hardening rounds after the
acceptance rows of a chunk prove.
