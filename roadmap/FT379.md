**FT379 (MEDIUM) — a record-only correction consumes no repair cycle, and its confirming round checks only the named findings.**

The bounded repair policy counts a correction that changes a disposition as a repair
cycle, even when the correction touches only the review record. A confirming round on such
a correction also grades all new prose, so each round can raise new low findings.

On 2026-10-05 the reviewer decided that a record-only change consumes no repair cycle. The
policy states that rule, and a confirming round on a record-only correction checks only the
findings that it names. A new preference in that prose is advice.
