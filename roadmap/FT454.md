**FT454 (MEDIUM) — a delegated full run reviews in unified mode by default, and a plan can change its review mode between chunks.**

A delegated `--full` run reviews each chunk with three axis sessions. In the FT450 build,
five paired unified confirming rounds gave the same verdict as three axis sessions for 39
percent of the tokens.

The reviewer decided on 2026-10-26 that unified review is the standard, and on 2026-10-27
that the default covers every chunk round, initial and confirming. The review record grades
each chunk's reviewer count against the plan's current review mode. A mid-build switch to
unified therefore invalidates every chunk that three axis sessions already reviewed. The
record must grade each chunk against the mode that held at its review. FT231 holds the
unified-review trial, and its "independent-session default" text goes stale with this row.
A unified form of `bench record review` reads one native report and records its three axis
sections in one call.
