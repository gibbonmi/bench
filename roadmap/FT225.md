**FT225 (MEDIUM, decision required) — reviewed landing has no path for a spec the review amended.**
Reviewed landing requires the staged spec to be byte-identical at source and
destination, because publication rewrites exactly those bytes to
`Status: implemented`. A review that changes a design decision therefore cannot
amend the spec in the source it reviewed: the amendment makes the landing refuse,
and the only way through is to land the amended spec on the destination first, by
hand, outside the flow.

That is a real workflow gap and the choice between its resolutions is the
reviewer's. Either an in-source spec amendment is legitimate and the landing
composes it — publishing the source's bytes rather than requiring agreement — or
it is illegitimate, review amendments belong on the destination, and the flow
should say so at the point of refusal and in the review phase's guidance. The
current state picks neither and refuses without naming the remedy.

Occurrence: 2026-08-18 bench-front-door landing — review rewrote story 34 and added a coverage row in the source; the landing refused on spec-byte inequality and the amendment was committed to the destination by hand under reviewer confirmation.
