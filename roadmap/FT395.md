**FT395 (LOW) — a spec-stage close lands its staged spec and states its outcome's deliverable binding.**

A spec-stage handoff called a run ready while the staged spec was off `main` and unbound,
so `bench commitment start` refused. The spec-stage close lands the staged spec and its
deliverable binding on `main` before the handoff. The handoff names the binding state.

The binding derives from the spec's `Roadmap:` line and its path, so `bench status`
prints the complete `bench commitment start` command. A ticket `Writes:` line owns
`.bench/commitment.json`, so the build preflight stays green after the binding.
