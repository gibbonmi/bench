**FT426 (MEDIUM, parked pending a repro) — `bench commitment plan` refuses a dependency that the order puts after its dependent.**

The plan validator refuses an unknown, self, or duplicate dependency and a cycle. It does
not compare a dependency with the outcome order. Admission refuses an outcome behind any
undelivered earlier outcome. A plan that orders a dependency after its dependent can
therefore validate and then deadlock. The outlook then reports `all-blocked` with no blocker
row, so it must also name why each outcome waits.

Graduate when `bench commitment plan --input <file>` with a dependency ordered after its
dependent exits 0, and `bench commitment show` then shows no outcome that can start.
