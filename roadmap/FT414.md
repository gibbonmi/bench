**FT414 (HIGH) — the record, commit, and landing verbs state their preconditions before an attempt.**

Agents learn the rules of the checkpoint, the record verbs, and the landing only from
refusals, one at a time. The verbs state their preconditions first. A read-only
`bench record status` lists the required chunk base, each stale or missing entry, and each
commit that breaks the chain. A `bench record next` prints the next required record or plan
action with its preconditions. `bench probe` and `bench test` take an `--out` option that
writes the complete response for the record verb.

`bench record status` also prints the plan rows of one chunk. A record verb that receives
two exclusive flags names the conflict. A read-only view prints the whole completion plan as
TOON: its chunks, tickets, verification ids, and assignments. `bench preflight build
--plan-only` prints the full row table. A landing refusal for a request mismatch names the
token that the commitment binding holds.

This row owns only the precondition projection. FT318 keeps the record imports, FT258 keeps
the commit change set, and FT401 keeps the refusal routes.
