refusals, one at a time. The verbs state their preconditions first. A read-only
`bench record status` lists the required chunk base, each stale or missing entry, and each
commit that breaks the chain. A `bench record next` prints the next required record or plan
action with its preconditions. `bench probe` and `bench test` take an `--out` option that
writes the complete response for the record verb.

`bench record status` also prints the plan rows of one chunk. A record verb that receives
two exclusive flags names the conflict.

and FT318 keep the record imports. FT258 keeps the commit change set, and FT401 keeps the
