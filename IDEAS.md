Quality risk to design against in that same row: slicing by seam optimizes local coherence and can manufacture cross-fence knowledge duplication — if no delegate owns the shared primitive, each seam writes its own local copy, which is exactly the Standards-1 defect (a failed-read predicate hand-derived at nine sites) that this review existed to fix.
