# Which changed-path families does the first census cover?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

Of 71 landings since 2026-10-18, 48 change no Go file. Of those 48, 30 change
only record paths, and 18 also change guidance under `.agents/`, `.bench/BENCH.md`,
or `docs/`. Does the first census cover only the record families, or the record
families and the guidance families? A family outside the census runs the whole
gate.

### Answer

The first reader table covers the record families and the guidance families.

- Record families: `specs/`, `roadmap/`, `ROADMAP.md`, `capture/`, `reviews/`,
  `decisions/`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and `.bench/commitment.json`.
- Guidance families: `.agents/`, `.bench/BENCH.md`, and `docs/`.

A landing that changes a path outside these families runs the whole gate. For
example, a change to `AGENTS.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, `projects/benchkit.md`, `.claude/`,
`bin/`, or `tests/` runs the whole gate.
