# What are the canonical terms for the census and the reduced schedule?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

`CONTEXT.md` gives "census" to the raw-call census, and the reader sweep entry
keeps the word away from every other meaning. The delta set is the one reduced
schedule today. Which canonical terms name the path-to-reader table and the new
reduced schedule, and which words go on their Avoid lists?

### Answer

The **reader table** is the one table that maps each covered non-Go path family
to the test packages and phases that read it. Avoid "census", "reader census",
and "input map".

The **reader set** is the reduced schedule that a landing runs when the reader
table covers every changed path. Avoid "reduced gate", "partial gate", and
"component partition". The delta set keeps its name and its chunk-checkpoint
meaning.

`CONTEXT.md` holds both terms.
