# Does FT356 ship as one spec or as two?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

FT356 holds two independently useful behaviors: the joins seam reduction and the
verb runner. Do they ship as one spec, or as two specs from this map?

### Answer

Two specs from this one map. The verb runner spec lands first. The seam
reduction spec lands second, and it rewrites each stubbed test once, directly
onto the verb runner.
