# Does the kit's CLAUDE.md take the managed form?

Blocked by: #1
Type: Grill

### Question

`bench link` writes a CLAUDE.md with one heading, one pointer sentence, and the two imports (`internal/adopt/marker.go:182`). The kit's own CLAUDE.md has nine lines, and most of them repeat the header of `AGENTS.md`. Does the kit's CLAUDE.md take the managed form?

### Answer

Yes. The kit's `CLAUDE.md` takes the managed form that `bench link` writes for a linked repository. That form holds one heading, one pointer sentence, and the imports of `AGENTS.md` and `.bench/BENCH.md`. The sentences that repeat the header of `AGENTS.md` go.
