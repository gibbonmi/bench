# What byte bound does BENCH.md get?

Blocked by: #6, #9, #10, #14, #15
Type: Grill

### Question

The moves of #9, #14, and #15 and the merge of #10 take `.bench/BENCH.md` from 15,797 bytes to about 11,500. What byte bound does it get?

### Answer

The limit of `.bench/BENCH.md` is its size in bytes after the edits of this outcome. That size is rounded up to the next multiple of 100 bytes, as #6 sets for every row. The estimate is about 11,600 bytes. Any later growth needs an edit of the budget table, which review sees.
