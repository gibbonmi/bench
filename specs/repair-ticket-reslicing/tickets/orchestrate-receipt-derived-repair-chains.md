## What to build

Make `/bench-implement-spec` consume the repair breakdown owned by `craft-tickets` without copying its slicing algorithm. Keep one repair ticket as the stated common case, send every member of a repair chain through ordinary assignment, checkpoint, and integration, and make the terminal repair ticket the proceed condition. Only after the complete chain lands may the original blocked assignment refresh with the original validated debug receipt. Extend the existing section-scoped workflow anchors and real-source mutation harness so omissions, the restored singular mandate, raw-Git routing, and premature refresh are attributable reds.
