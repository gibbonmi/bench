Restore the direct prohibition on taking any repair-ticket ownership fence from the blocked ticket's fence. Preserve every anchor and mutation that directly owns RS1-RS3, RO1-RO6, or CA1. Keep these edits together: a prose-only cut leaves old exact-literal anchors red, a registry-only cut leaves the finished-diagnostic inventory and table cases orphaned, and a harness-only cut leaves unmapped enforcement live; the coupled unit is the thinnest green removal.
