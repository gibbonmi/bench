## What to build

Create one focused policy reference that owns the two-cycle post-review allowance.
Link the line, implementation, and review guidance to that owner without copying its numeric limit.
Keep required work blocking, and retain optional advice outside native findings and repair-routing dispositions.

Retain cycle state in the existing pickup or handoff and refuse another cycle when evidence is ambiguous.
Extend the current anchor family and its independent expectations without weakening current review evidence.
Keep the compiled decision map unchanged because it also owns the future collection pilot.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Guidance permits at most two repair cycles after one chunk's initial review.
- [ ] Progress and a fresh review do not extend or reset the consumed allowance.
- [ ] Only an explicit reviewer decision permits repair work after exhaustion.
- [ ] Retained, full, delegated, unattended, and light-path work use the same allowance.
- [ ] Required checks, acceptance failures, correctness defects, safety defects, and mandatory standards remain blocking.
- [ ] Optional advice retains prose evidence without a disposition, finding ID, repair target, or finding count.
- [ ] A resumed author retains cycle state, and ambiguous evidence returns to the reviewer.
- [ ] Existing review and verification requirements still block stale, incomplete, or unresolved evidence.
- [ ] Removing a production policy anchor while retaining its independent expectation reds `TestImplementationContinuation` and restores cleanly.
- [ ] The implementation adds no pilot collection, detector, warning, counter schema, or validator change.
