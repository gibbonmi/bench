# What bounds repair and review work?

Blocked by: #2
Type: Grill

### Question

How many repair cycles may follow the initial review of one implementation chunk?
Does progress permit more cycles without a reviewer decision?

### Answer

Each implementation chunk permits at most two repair cycles after its initial review.
Progress does not extend this allowance.
A fresh review does not reset the count.
At the cap, unresolved blockers return to the reviewer.
