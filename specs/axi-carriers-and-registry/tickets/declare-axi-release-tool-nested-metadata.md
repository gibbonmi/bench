## What to build

The four remaining nested grammar owners export the membership they already hold
in their dispatch switches, and `cmd/bench` declares one AXI record per nested
member: the five `bench release` operations, the two `bench release-preflight`
modes, `bench gate pin`, the two `bench gate-go` subcommands, and both
`bench worktree-hook` operations. All twelve are declared explicitly non-AXI with
their grammar/help owner, renderer family, empty class, detail modes, and shared
routes.

`bench release-preflight` nests on `--mode`, not on a phase word. Its usage line
is `bench release-preflight --mode verify|publish [--profile public|bank]
[--phase <name>]`: `verify` and `publish` select the grammar and the required
flags, while `--phase` takes a value from `PhaseNames(mode)` and is a filter
within a mode rather than a surface of its own. So the two modes are the nested
members here, and no phase name is declared.

`bench worktree-hook` has two operations, not one: `WorktreeCommand` in
`internal/harness/worktree.go` accepts exactly `create` and `remove`, dispatching
`create` to `claudeCreate` and taking the `default` branch to `claudeRemove`. The
`default` spelling is a Go dispatch detail, not a missing member, so `remove` is
declared like every other operation.

Exporting each owner's list is what keeps this derived: a sixth release
operation, a third `gate-go` subcommand, or a third preflight mode makes the
declaration red until it is declared, and no parallel list of nested names
appears in `cmd/bench`. Whole-registry totality and byte-inertness stay with
`validate-axi-registry.md`, which is what lets this ticket land green alone.

## Acceptance

- [ ] [NT1] (covers CR5) each of the twelve release, release-preflight, gate, gate-go, and worktree-hook nested members exported by its owner package has exactly one declaration record carrying its complete metadata.
