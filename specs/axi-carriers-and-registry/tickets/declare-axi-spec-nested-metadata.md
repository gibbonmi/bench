# Declare AXI metadata for every spec nested member

Blocked by: declare-axi-query-root-metadata.md, declare-axi-operational-root-metadata.md
Ownership fence: `cmd/bench/command_registry.go`, `cmd/bench/command_registry_test.go`, `internal/spec`
Integration surfaces: declaration type and required-field validator→declare-axi-query-root-metadata.md; spec grammar owner→`internal/spec/build.go` (`buildOperationOrder`) and `internal/spec/spec.go` (the `Command` operation switch); parent root `spec` declaration→declare-axi-operational-root-metadata.md; completeness and inertness validation→validate-axi-registry.md
Contracts: the exported nested-member list crosses `internal/spec`→`cmd/bench/command_registry.go`, membership is the three `spec` operations plus the `spec build` family home and its nine lifecycle operations, order is `buildOperationOrder`, and an absent member is an error rather than an empty list, asserted by NS1 against the real exported list rather than a transcribed copy
Closure: NS1/spec-implemented, NS1/spec-retire, NS1/spec-history, NS1/spec-build-home, NS1/spec-build-start, NS1/spec-build-assign, NS1/spec-build-checkpoint, NS1/spec-build-integrate, NS1/spec-build-review, NS1/spec-build-status, NS1/spec-build-promote, NS1/spec-build-abandon, NS1/spec-build-reclaim

## What to build

`internal/spec` exports the nested grammar membership it already owns —
`implemented`, `retire`, `history` from the `Command` switch, and the nine
lifecycle operations from `buildOperationOrder` — and `cmd/bench` declares one
AXI record per nested member plus one for the `bench spec build` family home.
Every one is declared explicitly non-AXI with its grammar/help owner, renderer
family, empty class, detail modes, and shared routes.

The export is what makes this derived rather than transcribed: adding a tenth
lifecycle operation to `buildOperationOrder` makes the declaration red until it
is declared, and no second list of operation names is introduced anywhere.
Whole-registry totality and byte-inertness stay with `validate-axi-registry.md`,
which is what lets this ticket land green alone.

## Acceptance

- [ ] [NS1] (covers CR5) every nested `bench spec` member exported by `internal/spec`, plus the `bench spec build` family home, has exactly one declaration record carrying its complete metadata.
