## What to build

One conformance check derives every production member's AXI disposition from the
declaration in `cmd/bench` and compares it, as an order-independent set, against
an expectation authored independently in the check: exactly `anchors`,
`learnings`, `maps`, `guards`, `diff`, `coverage`, and `bench worktree list` are
AXI, and each of the other 74 members is an explicit exemption. Flipping the
disposition in either direction fails even though no command's bytes change,
which is the whole point: the current scope lives only in guidance and profile
prose, so nothing today can refuse a widened AXI claim.

Every member is graded on its own. The check runs one subtest per member —
`TestApprovedAXIDispositionPerMember/<member>` — so a widened claim names the
member that widened it rather than a set difference the reader has to diff by
hand, and so each of the 74 exemptions carries its own red rather than resting on
one representative flip. The 81-member universe is the 48 roots of
`commandRegistry` in `cmd/bench/main.go` plus the 33 nested members the sibling
declaration tickets land: the 8 worktree surfaces of
`declare-axi-worktree-nested-metadata.md`, the 13 spec members of
`declare-axi-spec-nested-metadata.md`, and the 12 release and tooling members of
`declare-axi-release-tool-nested-metadata.md`. The check enumerates the members
from the validated declaration rather than from a second list, so a member added
to any of those owners arrives ungraded-and-red instead of silently unexamined.

The expectation is the value that authorizes AXI enforcement, so it must not be
re-derived from the thing it grades. It is a literal name list in the check, and
DS1/independent-expectation makes reverting it to a self-derivation red.

Registering the check obliges its row in the `projects/benchkit.md` advertisement
table, which is why that file is on this ticket's fence; the profile's AXI
query-surface bullet already names the same seven surfaces and is now the
advertisement of an executable fact rather than the only statement of it.
