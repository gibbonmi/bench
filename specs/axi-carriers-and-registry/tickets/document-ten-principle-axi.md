## What to build

`craft-cli` documents principles 1 through 7 today and states the AXI scope in
prose that nothing checks. This ticket rewrites the guidance to state all ten
published principles in order, name the exact per-surface scope — the six
approved root queries plus `bench worktree list`, with every operational member
an explicit exemption — and record what is deferred rather than implied: no
`--fields`, no rendered `help[]`, and no content-first home for an operational
family. One concise `CHANGELOG.md` entry records the user-visible contract
statement. A docs-currency conformance check compares those claims against the
production declaration, so guidance can no longer drift from what the registry
declares; registering it obliges its row in the `projects/benchkit.md`
advertisement table, which is why that file is on this ticket's fence.

`.claude/skills/bench-craft-cli` is a symlink into `.agents/skills`, so editing
the skill in place satisfies the Claude mirror check with no second copy.
