# Declare AXI metadata for every operational root

Blocked by: declare-axi-query-root-metadata.md
Ownership fence: `cmd/bench/command_registry.go`, `cmd/bench/main.go`, `cmd/bench/command_registry_test.go`
Integration surfaces: declaration type and required-field validator→declare-axi-query-root-metadata.md; root member list→`cmd/bench/main.go` (`commandRegistry`); attachment enum→`cmd/bench/command_registry.go` (`processAttachment`); completeness and inertness validation→validate-axi-registry.md
Contracts: the per-member declaration record crosses `cmd/bench/main.go`→`cmd/bench/command_registry.go`, membership is every `commandRegistry` entry whose `Attachment` is `direct`, `system`, or `ship`, order is `commandRegistry` order, and no field may be absent, asserted by RO1 against the real `commandRegistry` slice rather than a sample list
Closure: RO1/version, RO1/worktree, RO1/resume-clean, RO1/session-inspect, RO1/shift, RO1/commit, RO1/spec, RO1/gate-go, RO1/guard-git, RO1/check-agent-line, RO1/setup, RO1/link, RO1/init, RO1/doctor, RO1/unlink, RO1/upgrade, RO1/worktree-hook, RO1/gate, RO1/gate-run, RO1/gate-pin, RO1/gate-phases, RO1/freshness-check, RO1/freshness-publish, RO1/canary, RO1/stop-verdict, RO1/release-preflight, RO1/prep-release, RO1/release

## What to build

Every operational root gets the same declaration record the query roots already
carry: the ten `direct` members, the fifteen `system` members, and the three
`ship` members of `commandRegistry` in `cmd/bench/main.go`. Each is declared
explicitly non-AXI with its grammar/help owner, renderer family, empty class,
detail modes, and applicable shared routes, because an operational member that
carries no disposition at all is exactly the drift `validate-axi-registry.md`
must be able to refuse.

Membership is derived per attachment class from the real `commandRegistry` slice,
so an operational root added later is red until it is declared. Whole-registry
totality and the byte-inertness proof stay with `validate-axi-registry.md`, which
is what lets this ticket land green alone.

## Acceptance

- [ ] [RO1] (covers CR5) every `direct`, `system`, and `ship` root in `commandRegistry` has exactly one declaration record carrying its complete metadata.
