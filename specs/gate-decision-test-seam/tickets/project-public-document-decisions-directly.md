## What to build

Replace the production-derived, per-case full-engine public-document matrix with one literal 11-file/eight-directory expectation table exercised directly through `scopeComponentsForGeneration`. The table compares the whole decision answer, proves its membership equals the production-declared inventory without deriving any row expectation from production, captures at most one generation per changed state, restores fixture bytes directly, and leaves seeded slot evidence unchanged. Keep the representative full-engine projection, decision-site fail-closed, and evaluation materialization tests as unchanged regression controls. This is one atomic diff because the literal table, its membership tripwire, the direct-decision loop, and restoration accounting share one fixture lifecycle; landing a thinner cut either leaves the table unused and compile-red or retains a second production-derived/full-engine exhaustive loop that defeats the ticket's outcome.
