# Where must missing or stale assurance block completion?

Blocked by: #7, #8
Type: Grill

### Question

Where must missing or stale assurance block completion?
Choose the evidence owner and the effect on a completion claim.
Distinguish phase reporting from FT200's unresolved landing chokepoint.
Keep the gate as the single oracle.

### Answer

Retain source-bound review results in the existing review and completion records. Missing or stale required evidence blocks advancement to the next chunk and completion of the spec.

The gate owns the mechanical evidence check. Do not add a separate approval system or another done-oracle. A review record establishes that a review occurred for its named source. It cannot mechanically prove that the review judgment is correct.

Reviewer confirmed this decision in the shaping conversation.
