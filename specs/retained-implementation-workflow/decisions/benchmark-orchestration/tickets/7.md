# What assurance must completion evidence establish?

Blocked by: #1
Type: Grill

### Question

What assurance must completion evidence establish?

### Answer

Completion evidence identifies the source each check or review examined, its performer, terminal state, and outcome. Record each of the three review axes, including reviews with no findings. Distinguish author-run tests and probes from independent reviews.

Keep review coverage valid through implementation repairs. Missing, stale, skipped, and failed evidence remain distinct. A pending check supplies no completed result. Ticket #10 determines how missing or stale assurance affects completion.

Reviewer confirmed this decision in the shaping conversation.
