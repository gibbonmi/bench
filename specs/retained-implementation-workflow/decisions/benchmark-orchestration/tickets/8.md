# How are the independently useful outcomes split?

Blocked by: #1
Type: Grill

### Question

How are the independently useful outcomes split?

### Answer

Deliver four independently useful outcomes through separate specs:

1. Implementation workflow: the retained session, chunks, authority, model recommendations, and review cadence.
2. Continuation: progress, stall responses, and stop conditions.
3. Completion evidence: accurate verification and review records.
4. Assessment: complete cost and quality comparisons under FT231.

Keep the existing roadmap owners. A proposed test, probe, or evidence-retrieval improvement enters a spec only after its remaining gap is reproduced.

Reviewer confirmed this decision in the shaping conversation.
