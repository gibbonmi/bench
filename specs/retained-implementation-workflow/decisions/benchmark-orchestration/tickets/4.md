# Who can change chunk boundaries during implementation?

Blocked by: #12
Type: Grill

### Question

Who can change chunk boundaries during implementation?

### Answer

The implementation session can split, combine, or reorder chunks without another approval request. Preserve the approved behavior, dependency requirements, acceptance coverage, and a review checkpoint for each resulting chunk. Complete the required review and repairs before advancing to the next chunk.

Update the chunk plan so reviewers can see the resulting boundaries. Ticket #5 owns the expansion log and its drain route.

Reviewer confirmed this decision in the shaping conversation.
