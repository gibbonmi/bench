# What assessment record and trial rule support adoption?

Blocked by: #8, #9
Type: Grill

### Question

What assessment record and trial rule support adoption?
Choose storage, granularity, retention, cost cutoffs, cases, repetition, and quality tolerances.
Preserve FT231's division between Bench measures and harness measures.
Distinguish an exploratory pilot from evidence that permits a default change.

### Answer

Keep one local machine-readable assessment record per run. Include chunk and role breakdowns, with links to native evidence. Retain the records until explicit cleanup.

Include failed attempts, repairs, reviews, and verification. Separate cached input, uncached input, and output tokens. Distinguish estimated cost from actual charges, and preserve unknown charges as unknown.

Collect evidence from ordinary work. Bench supplies its reliable elapsed-time, iteration, census, and diff measures. The harness supplies its reliable token, tool-call, file-read, and turn measures. A measure without a reliable source stays absent.

A paid comparison trial requires an approved plan. That plan fixes tasks, repetitions, execution conditions, quality tolerances, and the total budget before execution. Use held-out tasks and report variation. Preserve FT231's three-arm comparison for causal claims about a kit capability.

An exploratory pilot does not establish a default. Ticket #9 owns the evidence criterion and reviewer authority for later default changes.

Reviewer confirmed this decision in the shaping conversation.
