# How does the approved scope divide into specs?

Blocked by: #5, #6, #7, #8, #9
Type: Grill

### Question

Does the reviewer want one spec or separate specs for independently useful outcomes?

### Answer

The scope divides into five specs: delegate preparation and review dispatch; landing completion; recoverable reset; diagnostic improvements; and delegation trials. All five specs use this decision map as their shared decision source.
