## What to build

Roots are not the whole public grammar: eight owners declare 32 nested operations
behind seven registry roots. This ticket derives them — the eight worktree modes
from `internal/usage/worktree.go`, the three ordinary spec operations from
`internal/spec/spec.go`, the nine spec-build operations from `buildOperationOrder`
in `internal/spec/build.go`, the five publication operations from
`internal/publication/command.go`, the two release-preflight modes from
`internal/preflight/command.go`, the two `gate-go` steps from
`internal/gate/gate_go.go`, `gate pin` from `internal/gate/gate.go`, and the two
`worktree-hook` modes from `internal/harness` — and lands each under the stable
case ID `nested-<root>-<operation>`.

`cmd/bench/axi_compatibility_test.go` proves each derived member is live rather
than transcribed: it drives the real dispatcher through `Command{}.Run` and
requires the operation's own refusal or acceptance, not the family home's
unknown-operation list. That distinction is the point — a member the census
invents would answer with the family list, and a member the census drops would
never be compared at all.

## Acceptance

- [ ] [NG1] (covers CO3) the derived nested census contains every operation the eight declared nested grammar owners expose, each proven live by the real dispatcher answering as that operation rather than as its family home, and names any operation the dispatcher answers that the census omits.
