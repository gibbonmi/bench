# Which verbs look up a grade first, and what does a hit skip?

Blocked by: #1, #10
Type: Grill

### Question

The criterion `FT314-gate-reuse.grade-key` states that each verb looks up a grade before it checks its own conditions.
The candidate verbs are `bench gate` in each form, `bench worktree land`, `bench worktree merge`, and the commit lane.
Decide which verbs look up a grade, the point in each verb where the lookup occurs, and the work that a hit skips.

### Answer

Every verb that can run a whole gate looks up a whole grade of its graded tree first.
These verbs are `bench gate` in each form, `bench worktree land`, `bench worktree merge` to a target with no lane, and `bench commit` in a repository with no lane.
The lookup comes after the verb resolves its graded tree, and before it picks a reader set or a delta set.

A hit skips every gate phase.
The verb still runs its own purpose and evidence checks (ticket #1) and its publication or commit step.
`--fresh` skips the lookup, as it skips a reusable green today.
A verb with a declared lane keeps its lane and does no lookup.
