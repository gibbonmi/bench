# Does FT314 ship the merge plan disclosure?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

The closed decision of 2026-10-26 fixes the disclosure form as `bench worktree merge --plan`.
The FT314 criteria do not name a disclosure.
Since FT335, a kit merge runs the target's lane, so a whole-gate merge occurs only in a repository with no lane.
Decide whether the plan form ships in this outcome or becomes its own intake.

### Answer

FT314 ships no merge plan disclosure.
The `bench worktree merge --plan` form, closed on 2026-10-26, becomes its own intake through `bench idea`.
The spec drops the merge plan stories, rows, and chunk of the draft.
