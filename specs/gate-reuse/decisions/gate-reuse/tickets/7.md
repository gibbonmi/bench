# What does the grade key save on checkpoint-then-landing compositions and retries?

Blocked by: #6
Type: Research

### Question

Run the measurement that ticket #6 designs, and record the result as a map asset.

### Answer

The asset `specs/gate-reuse/decisions/gate-reuse/assets/saving-measurement.md` holds the cited result.
In 19 days, 30 spec landings each reproduced the tree of their final complete checkpoint, at a median of 288 s for each whole gate.
The upper bound is about 53 minutes per week.
In 20 of 27 session records, the complete checkpoint ran in a worktree with its own build.
So the key of ticket #3 alone reuses only about 14 minutes per week.
Retries add no saving, because a retry on an unchanged composition already reuses its green in one checkout.
