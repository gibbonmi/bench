# What does the schedule member name?

Blocked by: #1
Type: Grill

### Question

The criterion `FT314-gate-reuse.grade-key` names "the schedule" as a key member.
The draft reads it as the bytes of the baseline phase manifest, which select the phases of a run.
Two other green records exist: the delta green in `<git-dir>/bench-last-delta`, and the reader record of a reader-set landing.
Decide whether the schedule member names only the phase manifest bytes.
The other choice also names the executed schedule (whole, delta set, or reader set), so that one store holds each grade.

### Answer

The schedule member is the bytes of the baseline phase manifest, as the draft rows for the baseline schedule state.
An absent manifest and an empty manifest give two values.
The grade store holds whole grades only.
The delta green and the reader record stay their own record classes, and neither answers a grade lookup.
