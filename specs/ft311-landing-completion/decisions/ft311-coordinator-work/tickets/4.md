# How do lower-tier routes begin?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

Do lower-tier routes begin as bounded trials rather than blanket defaults?

### Answer

Cheap-tier routes begin as bounded trials. Exact evidence collection and covered narrow repairs use cheap models at low effort during those trials. Repair triage and semantic review retain the mid tier. Kit guidance authoring retains the mid tier at high effort. Prose diagnostics become automatic; an edit that affects guidance retains mid-tier authorship at high effort. These trials do not change the project tier defaults.
