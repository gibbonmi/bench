# Where does the single-read census draw its edge?

Blocked by: #4, #9
Type: Grill

### Question

Six exported functions are not verb entries, but they read the Bench home or the
clock themselves. Packages outside the worktree package call them. Do they count
as entries for the census in ticket 4? Two kit-root reads exist, and their
fallbacks differ on purpose. What does the verb entry read, and what does it
pass down?

### Answer

A top-tier consultation decided this ticket at the reviewer's direction.

- Every exported function of the worktree package is a census entry. It may read
  the Bench home and the clock at most once each in its own body, and it passes
  the values down. Callers in other packages keep their signatures.
- The census refuses a home or clock read in an unexported function. It also
  refuses a call from an unexported source function to an exported function
  that reads them. The spec lifts the read in `claimRecordedLease` into the
  exported `ClaimRecordedLease`.
- One gate-exported reader reads the raw `BENCH_KIT` value, and `kitRoot` and
  `KitDir` also use it. The verb entry calls that reader once and passes the
  value down.
- The gate package gets lane and kit-source functions that take the kit value.
  Each keeps its own fallback for an empty value: `kitRoot` falls back to the
  graded root, and `KitDir` to the executable's parent. The present functions
  stay as wrappers, so their other callers do not change.
- The seam reduction spec fixes the three latent defects in the classification
  asset. One acceptance row runs resume-clean under a non-default home through
  the verb runner.
