# What fixed schema does a claim carry?

Blocked by: #2, #3
Type: Grill

### Question

What enumerated status, confidence field, and post-verification label does
each claim carry, so that a retro can aggregate the pairs without prose
repair?

### Answer

Each claim carries three fields. `status` is one of `verified`, `claimed`,
or `abstained`. `verified` means the author ran the named check and returns
its red-to-green log. `claimed` means the author asserts the outcome without
an executed check.

`confidence` is the integer from 0 to 10, and it is absent when the status
is `abstained`. `label` is `held` or `refuted`, and only a label source from
ticket 4 writes it. No free-text field enters the record.
