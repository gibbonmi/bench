# Does confidence change whether a review finding blocks?

Blocked by: #2
Type: Grill

### Question

A finding blocks today on its kind and its citation. Does a stated
confidence on the finding change whether it blocks, or is it recorded only?

### Answer

A stated confidence never changes whether a finding blocks. A finding blocks
on its kind and its citation, as the one-round rule states. The confidence is
recorded in the review record and scored after the reviewer's disposition.
