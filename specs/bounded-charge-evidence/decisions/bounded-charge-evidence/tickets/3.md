# Which delivery and lifecycle contract preserves that completeness?

Blocked by: #2
Type: Grill

### Question

Which delivery design preserves complete pinned evidence across bounded responses?

### Answer

The reviewer selects an immutable prepared evidence artifact with stateless bounded reads on 2026-09-15.
Bench explicitly prepares that artifact in repository-common storage.
The preflight owner serves its bounded pages.
The artifact preserves generated review bytes without recomputation during retrieval.
The artifact remains readable across worktrees and after its originating assignment releases.

Historical retrieval does not permit action against a moved source.
Current-action validation retains the source and assignment checks.
The design adds no server-maintained consumer-progress session.

The [comparison](../assets/research.md#q6-distinct-designs) records the alternatives and their tradeoffs.
The detailed candidate grammar, framing, and resource policies remain proposals where later tickets have not resolved them.
