# What does consumer verification prove before action?

Blocked by: #3
Type: Grill

### Question

Should verification prove complete byte coverage and current source binding while phase guidance requires approval, the supplement, and required context?
The recommendation preserves the honest-agent trust contract.
A receipt cannot prove comprehension or prevent arbitrary editor use.

### Answer

Before action, the phase requires complete verified delivery, current source checks, reviewer approval, and the task supplement.
Required context must remain available to the acting consumer.
Artifact integrity does not prove delivery, approval, or comprehension.
The change does not introduce a system that blocks editing across harnesses.
