## What to build

After the existing pool-path and follow-on verdicts, an eligible ordinary raw read stays allowed with exit 0, and stderr names the matching native file or line-range form.
Eligibility is structural through the existing shellcommand observations, projection owners, and routine-prefix resolver.
It covers direct named-file cat, head, tail, and a simple read-only sed line print with an unambiguous path and range.
Examples, stdin-only calls, unknown parses, transformations, other sed programs, and unresolved operands get no advice.

The adapter supplies a verified assignment through `intent.AssignmentForWorktree`; primary or unowned reads get no attribution.
The census owner combines advice observations with pool-path attribution, so one invocation appends at most one raw-call record in the existing format.
The guard fixtures that pin empty stderr for an eligible read change in place.
Append to the census account in BENCH-reference.md; do not rewrite its anchored sentence.
