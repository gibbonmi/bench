## What to build

`status.Command` constructs a shared outcome carrying its own kind and exit for each of its three result classes before the existing board renderer runs, and every `status.Signal` gains a typed action carrying the ordered whitespace tokens of its `action` cell together with the `status.StepSeparator` step boundaries the board already writes. The typing is additive: `Signal.Action` keeps its exact current string, `render` keeps emitting the same `action` cells, and no typed metadata reaches stdout. Deciding whether an action *qualifies* as invocable stays with `internal/handoff`'s `IsInvocable`; this ticket only supplies the structure that decision reads.

`migrate-structure-outcome.md` is OA3's accountable first claimant, under the tie-break rule that accountability goes to the ticket covering the spec map's first-enumerated OA3 family (structure, before models, testreport, guards, outline, roadmap, status, handoff, dashboard, and worktree query), so this ticket claims OA3 as defense in depth.

This ticket is OA4's accountable first claimant under the same enumeration-order rule: it covers OA4's first-enumerated family (status, before handoff and dashboard). `migrate-handoff-outcome-action.md` and `migrate-dashboard-outcome-composition.md` claim OA4 as defense in depth, and the dashboard ticket additionally owns the composition rows neither producer can assert alone.

Refresh precondition (OA3 and OA4 are not TDD-able until OA1 and the prerequisite carriers land): `internal/axi` must exist as Go package `axi` with `go doc ./internal/axi Outcome` and `go doc ./internal/axi Action` both resolving, and `migrate-output-adapter.md` must have landed so `cmd/bench/main.go`'s `outputCommand` consumes a carrier. `cmd/bench/axi_compatibility_test.go` is an unchanged input this ticket only runs.

Write two owner tests in `internal/status/status_test.go`: `TestStatusOutcomeRouteByResultClass`, one subtest per result class driving the real `status.Command` over a `t.TempDir()` git fixture and asserting the constructed outcome's kind and exit; and `TestBoardActionTyping`, driving the real `status.SignalsWith` over clean, dirty, and stale-gate fixtures and asserting the typed action's tokens, step boundaries, and order.

## Acceptance

- [ ] [ST1] (covers OA3) `status.Command` constructs a shared outcome with its own kind and exit for each of its three result classes, with byte-identical stdout and exits.
- [ ] [ST2] (covers OA4) every `status.Signal` carries a typed action holding its action's ordered token vector and its `status.StepSeparator` step boundaries, in the board's severity order, with byte-identical `action` cells.
