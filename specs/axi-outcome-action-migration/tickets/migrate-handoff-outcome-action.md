## What to build

`handoff.Command` constructs a shared outcome carrying its own kind and exit for each of its seven result classes before the pin block is rendered or written, and `nextAction` carries the status producer's typed action forward instead of re-deriving one from the `Signal.Action` string. `IsInvocable` keeps deciding qualification from `harnessPrefix[harnessClaude]`, `benchCommandPrefix`, and the `status.StepSeparator` compound rule — it now reads those facts off the typed action rather than re-splitting the string. `translate` still rewrites the harness prefix for the rendered bytes, and every rendered byte of the pin block and `capture/session-handoff.md` is unchanged.

`migrate-structure-outcome.md` is OA3's accountable first claimant, under the tie-break rule that accountability goes to the ticket covering the spec map's first-enumerated OA3 family (structure, before models, testreport, guards, outline, roadmap, status, handoff, dashboard, and worktree query), so this ticket claims OA3 as defense in depth.

`migrate-status-outcome-action.md` is OA4's accountable first claimant under the same enumeration-order rule (status, before handoff and dashboard), so this ticket claims OA4 as defense in depth.

Refresh precondition (OA3 and OA4 are not TDD-able until OA1 and the prerequisite carriers land): `internal/axi` must exist as Go package `axi` with `go doc ./internal/axi Outcome` and `go doc ./internal/axi Action` both resolving, and `migrate-output-adapter.md` and `migrate-status-outcome-action.md` must have landed so `status.Signal` already carries its typed action. `cmd/bench/axi_compatibility_test.go` is an unchanged input this ticket only runs.

Write two owner tests: `TestHandoffOutcomeRouteByResultClass` in a new `internal/handoff/handoff_test.go`, one subtest per result class driving the real `handoff.Command` over `t.TempDir()` fixtures and asserting the constructed outcome's kind and exit; and `TestHandoffNextActionTyping` in `internal/handoff/facts_test.go`, driving the real `status.SignalsWith` producer through `nextAction` over invocable, compound, and prose-only board fixtures.

## Acceptance

- [ ] [HA1] (covers OA3) `handoff.Command` constructs a shared outcome with its own kind and exit for each of its seven result classes, with byte-identical stdout, written document, and exits.
- [ ] [HA2] (covers OA4) `nextAction` carries the status producer's typed action forward, keeps `IsInvocable`'s prefix and compound-sequence disposition, and carries zero actions when the board offers none, with byte-identical pin bytes.
