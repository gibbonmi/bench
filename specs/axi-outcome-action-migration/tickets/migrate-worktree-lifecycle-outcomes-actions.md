## What to build

Each of the seven worktree lifecycle modes routed by `worktreeCommand` in `cmd/bench/main.go` — `create` (`CreateCommand`), `list` (`ListCommand`), `refresh` (`refresh.Refresh`/`RenderRefresh`), `cleanup` (`CleanCommand`), `release` (`ReleaseCommand`), `recovery` (`RecoveryCommand`), and `resume` (`ResumeCleanCommand`) — constructs a shared outcome carrying its own lifecycle kind and exit, and each recovery/cleanup next step becomes a typed action carrying its ref and fingerprint as fixed arguments. Authority does not move: `fingerprintRecoveryUnder` keeps sealing its full declared preimage under `recoveryFingerprintDomain` and `recoveryFingerprintEffects`, `PlanRecovery` keeps deciding retire versus discard, and no action is derived before the plan's fingerprint exists. The list mode's outcome route is `migrate-worktree-query-outcome.md`'s; this ticket only adds the lifecycle empty-form claim over it.

Refresh precondition (OA7 is not TDD-able until OA1 and the prerequisite carriers land): `internal/axi` must exist as Go package `axi` with `go doc ./internal/axi Outcome` and `go doc ./internal/axi Action` both resolving, and both blockers must have landed. `cmd/bench/axi_compatibility_test.go` is an unchanged input this ticket only runs.

Write the owner test `TestWorktreeLifecycleOutcomeRoutePerMode` in `internal/worktree/lifecycle_route_test.go`: one subtest per closure token driving the real mode entry over a `t.TempDir()` git fixture, and `TestWorktreeRecoveryFingerprintPreimage` in `internal/worktree/recovery_plan_test.go` for the two authority rows, both alongside the existing `TestRecoveryFingerprintSealsTheChangeSummary` control.
