## What to build

Each of the nine spec-build operations dispatched by `executeBuild` in `cmd/bench/specbuild.go` constructs a shared outcome carrying its own lifecycle kind and exit, and each `Status.Next` becomes a typed action before `renderStatus` writes the `spec_build` row. Usage refusals from `spec.ParseBuild` keep exit 2 and precondition refusals from `buildError` keep exit 1 — the outcome carries those, it never re-derives them. Orchestration prose that names no invokable command (the release-assignment and review-handoff `next` values) stays non-invokable, and the one-row no-run `status` and the empty sections of `--full` status keep their exact current forms. This spec emits no `help[]`.

Refresh precondition (OA5 is not TDD-able until the prerequisite carriers land): `internal/axi` must exist as Go package `axi` with `go doc ./internal/axi Outcome` and `go doc ./internal/axi Action` both resolving, from `specs/axi-carriers-and-registry/tickets/introduce-outcome-carrier.md` and `introduce-action-carrier.md`. `cmd/bench/axi_compatibility_test.go` is an unchanged input this ticket only runs.

Write two owner tests: `TestSpecBuildOutcomeRoutePerOperation` in `cmd/bench/specbuild_test.go`, driving `executeBuild` against a fake `buildService` for each of the nine operations under `context.WithTimeout(ctx, bounds.TestDeadline(4*time.Second))`; and `TestSpecBuildActionTyping` in the same file for the action and empty classes.

## Acceptance

- [ ] [SB1] (covers OA5) each of the nine spec-build operations constructs a shared outcome carrying its own lifecycle kind and its existing usage/refusal exit, and each `Status.Next` becomes a typed action keeping its fixed inputs and its invocable-versus-orchestration-prose disposition, with the no-run and `--full` empty forms and every other byte unchanged.
