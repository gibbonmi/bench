## What to build

`dashboard.Command` constructs a shared outcome carrying its own kind and exit for each of its six result classes, and `gather` composes the producers' typed facts rather than recomputing any of them: the severity-ordered signal vector and each signal's typed action come from `internal/status`, and the gate verdict comes from `status.GateVerdict(root)` rather than a local read of `<git-dir>/bench-last-gate`. Every byte of the rendered page, the written `bench-dashboard.html`, and the printed path is unchanged, and this spec emits no `help[]`.

This is the junction for OA4: neither producer can assert on its own that its typed facts survive composition, so the composition rows live here and are asserted against the real producers rather than a fixture standing in for them.

`migrate-structure-outcome.md` is OA3's accountable first claimant, under the tie-break rule that accountability goes to the ticket covering the spec map's first-enumerated OA3 family (structure, before models, testreport, guards, outline, roadmap, status, handoff, dashboard, and worktree query), so this ticket claims OA3 as defense in depth. `migrate-status-outcome-action.md` is OA4's accountable first claimant under the same rule (status, before handoff and dashboard), so this ticket claims OA4 as defense in depth while owning the composition half of the row.

Refresh precondition (OA3 and OA4 are not TDD-able until OA1 and the prerequisite carriers land): `internal/axi` must exist as Go package `axi` with `go doc ./internal/axi Outcome` and `go doc ./internal/axi Action` both resolving, and `migrate-output-adapter.md`, `migrate-status-outcome-action.md`, and `migrate-handoff-outcome-action.md` must have landed so both producers already emit typed facts. `cmd/bench/axi_compatibility_test.go` is an unchanged input this ticket only runs.

Write two owner tests in `internal/dashboard/dashboard_test.go`: `TestDashboardOutcomeRouteByResultClass`, one subtest per result class driving the real `dashboard.Command` and asserting the constructed outcome's kind and exit; and `TestTypedProducerFactsReachTheDashboard`, driving the real `status.SignalsWith` and `status.GateVerdict` producers through `gather` over clean, active, and stale fixtures.
