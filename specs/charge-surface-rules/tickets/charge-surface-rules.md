## What to build

The author and repair charge surface states four reviewer-approved rules, each in one source.
Before the ticket commit, every author charge and repair charge requires a duplicated-facts sweep of the delta and a recorded red per independent expectation.
The delegation discipline states this rule once. The final-attempt retry rule and the `bench-writer` agent point at it.

The repair-charge template names the debug route and the repair context that the coordinator read before the charge.
The operating guide lets an in-scope repair fence expansion continue without a stop, with a plan commit and a `bench learning` entry.
The debug phase names the ticket author as the loop owner before review, and the fresh repair session after review.
Anchors pin each new rule, and the DG1 canaries follow the new owner sentences.
