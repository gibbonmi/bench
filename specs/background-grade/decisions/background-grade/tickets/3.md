# Which consumers does a background grade serve, and does FT451 split?

Blocked by: #1
Type: Grill

### Question

A chunk checkpoint and a landing need different trees and different running executables.
The chunk consumer serves the build of a staged spec. The landing consumer serves a spec-less or tickets-only worktree.
Each consumer is useful alone. Does one outcome serve both, or does FT451 split?

### Answer

One FT451 outcome serves two consumers, and FT451 does not split.
The reviewer chose this on 2026-10-27.

- A chunk checkpoint of the build of a staged spec finds the background green of its subject and runs no second gate.
- A landing of a spec-less or tickets-only kit worktree finds the background green of its subject and runs no second gate.
- That landing hit needs a whole-gate landing plan (ticket 15) and a destination that did not move after the grade started (ticket 12).
- The complete checkpoint keeps its own run. Its green keeps answering the landing of the same spec, as ADR 0031 states.

The lookup criterion of `FT451-background-grade` stays as admitted, so no `bench commitment plan` change follows.
The spec can order the chunk consumer before the landing consumer, because both share the trigger, the bound, and the wait rule.
