# What does the gate reuse machinery do for a background grade?

Blocked by: none
Type: Research

### Question

Which current facts bind a background grade of a lane-passed tip?
Cover the grade key, the evidence store, the checkout cache and lock, the lookup order, and the subject of each consumer.
Cover the subject members that read the live worktree, the two run-binary selections, and the process lifetime rules.

### Answer

The research asset `../assets/research.md` holds the cited facts in its sections Q1 to Q5 and Q7.

- The evidence store keeps one green for each grade key in the git common directory, so every worktree shares it.
- Each checkout keeps its own gate cache and gate lock. A pending cache blocks reuse, and a held lock makes a run refuse with no wait.
- The chunk checkpoint grades the HEAD tree without the review record, in its own process. The complete checkpoint and the landing grade the published tree under the installed broker.
- A prospective subject reads the graded tree from a private checkout. It also reads the baseline schedule and the baseline runner digest from the live worktree.
- The identity selects the run-binary source from the presence of the prospective script. The run selects it from the resolved kind. The answers differ only when `BENCH_KIT` names another checkout.
- A process that outlives its verb starts through the lifetime owner and needs a census row under ADR 0030.
- A record commit keeps the chunk key, and a plan commit moves it.
