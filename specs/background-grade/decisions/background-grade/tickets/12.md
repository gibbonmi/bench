# What stays out of scope?

Blocked by: #3
Type: Grill

### Question

Which neighbouring changes stay out of FT451, so that later sessions do not reopen them?

### Answer

The reviewer confirmed these ten exclusions on 2026-10-27.

1. A new grade when the destination moves. A landing after a destination move runs its own gate.
2. A background grade of the complete checkpoint subject.
3. The FT255 shared pool and any change of a foreground width.
4. The FT448 rerun cost of a repair record.
5. The FT372 family of nested gates that outlive their probe.
6. A landing stack under ADR 0032 as a consumer.
7. A landing that reads a delta green.
8. The GR44 edge between two foreground runs.
9. A change of the ADR 0031 rule that the chunk checkpoint keeps its own process.
10. A freshness window longer than 60 minutes for a background green.

Each item has its own owner or a closed decision, so FT451 stays the background grade and its bound.
