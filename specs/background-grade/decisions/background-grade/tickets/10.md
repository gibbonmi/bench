# What happens to the prepared run and its guidance?

Blocked by: #3, #6
Type: Grill

### Question

The `--prepare` form and two guidance points in `/bench-implement-spec` start a prepared chunk run by hand.
When a background grade serves the chunk checkpoint, does the prepared run stay, or does FT451 retire it?

### Answer

FT451 retires the prepared run.
The reviewer chose this on 2026-10-27.

- The `--prepare` form leaves `bench gate`, its usage line, and its refusal reruns.
- The two guidance points in `/bench-implement-spec` that start a prepared run leave, with the instruction to wait on its PID.
- The prepared-run lines of the bounded repair policy and of `.bench/BENCH-reference.md` leave.
- The background grade covers each chunk freeze and each repair commit, and no hand step starts it.

One mechanism then grades a chunk subject ahead of its checkpoint.
