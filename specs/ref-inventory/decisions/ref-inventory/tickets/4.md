# How does the inventory handle a ref that a spec retirement or a re-authored landing superseded?

Blocked by: #1, #3
Type: Grill

### Question

Three live roots are FT336 ticket drafts whose ticket landed later under a re-authored commit.
The 2026-09-17 debug-loop retirement left 22 superseded worktrees with no release path.
No content proof can see a supersession.
How does the inventory handle such a ref?

### Answer

A superseded ref classifies as unique, because no proof exists.
Only an explicit reviewer discard removes it, through the route ticket 6 fixes, and that discard writes the discarded ref.
`bench spec retire` lists candidates as ticket 9 fixes, and it discards nothing.
