## What to build

`outline.Command` stops slicing `rows = rows[:emitted]` itself and instead hands the complete scanned row set plus its owner policy — the `bounds.OutlineRowLimit` cap in default mode, no cap under `--full`, and the entry counting unit — to the shared `internal/axi` bounded-projection carrier. Every metadata fact stays owner-derived from the scan rather than from the visible rows: `tracked_files` from `len(files)`, `scanned_files` from the scan counter, `skipped_files` from `len(skips)`, `total_symbols` from the pre-projection symbol count, `emitted_symbols` from the projection's selection, `omitted_symbols` from total minus emitted, and `truncated` from `omitted > 0 || len(skips) > 0`. The `outline[N]{...}`, `outline_meta`, and `outline_skips` TOON tables emit byte-identical output.

Tree condition at refresh time: the package directory `internal/axi` exists and exports `axi.Projection`, the CR3 carrier holding selected content plus owner-supplied total, emitted, omitted, truncated, completeness, and counting unit without inferring any of them (the symbol name is fixed by the carriers spec). Confirm with `go doc ./internal/axi Projection` before starting; if the package or symbol is absent, stop and report rather than inventing a local shim.

The row projection and the metadata facts stay in one ticket because they are computed in one `Command` body from one scan: a split that lands the projection first would leave `emitted`, `omitted`, and `truncated` reading a selection the metadata half does not yet see, which is the exact `TestCommandBoundsRowsAndFullRetainsMetadata` meta-row red no thinner cut can land green through.
