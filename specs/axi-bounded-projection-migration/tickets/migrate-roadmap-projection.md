## What to build

`internal/roadmap`'s `limited` becomes a thin owner adapter over the shared `internal/axi` bounded-projection carrier: the owner still supplies the 4096-byte cap, the byte counting unit, the UTF-8 validity backoff, `len([]byte(s))` as the original total, and the uncapped `full` disposition, and the carrier performs the selection. All nine `limited` call sites in `context_parse.go` keep calling `limited`, so every projected field — `RoadmapRow.Body`/`BodyBytes`/`Truncated`, `IdeaFact.Text`/`TextBytes`/`Truncated`, `LearningFact.Body`/`BodyBytes`/`Truncated`, `RetroFact` body/bytes/truncated, and every `ParseFailure` raw/bytes/truncated — carries byte-identical values, and an already-bounded value is never projected a second time.

Tree condition at refresh time: the package directory `internal/axi` exists and exports `axi.Projection`, the CR3 carrier holding selected content plus owner-supplied total, emitted, omitted, truncated, completeness, and counting unit without inferring any of them (the symbol name is fixed by the carriers spec). Confirm with `go doc ./internal/axi Projection` before starting; if the package or symbol is absent, stop and report rather than inventing a local shim.

The nine call sites stay in one ticket because they share the single `limited` body: a per-site split leaves the other sites on the old local slice while `internal/conformance`'s residue census (contract-projection-routes.md) still finds the removed selector, and no per-site cut lands an independently green production change of its own.
