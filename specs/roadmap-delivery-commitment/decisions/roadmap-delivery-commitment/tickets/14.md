# What bounds the first quality milestone?

Blocked by: #8, #11, #13
Type: Grill

### Question

Does the first milestone cover FT373 and the remaining September 29 survey outcomes, or every current defect and refactoring row?

### Answer

The first milestone covers FT376, FT373, and FT349, in that order. The September 29 survey outcomes stay uncommitted intake for a later plan. Verify which obligations have already shipped before scheduling the remaining work. Unrelated later feature additions stay outside the milestone. Minimal supporting work needed for an approved fix or refactor remains within that outcome.
