# What happens to a background grade when its worktree lands, is released, or is cleaned?

Blocked by: #6, #7
Type: Grill

### Question

A background grade outlives the verb that started it.
What does a landing, a `bench worktree release`, or a `bench worktree clean` do to the running grade and the queued grade of its worktree?

### Answer

A worktree verb stops the grade of its worktree, except a landing of the same key.
The reviewer chose this on 2026-10-27, and widened it after the map review.

- A landing whose grade key equals the running grade's key waits for that grade, as ticket 6 states.
- A landing of another key stops the running grade of its worktree and drops its queued grade before it grades.
- `bench worktree release`, `bench worktree clean`, and `bench worktree reclaim` each stop the running grade and drop the queued grade of each worktree that they remove.
- A landing stack stops the running grade and drops the queued grade of each member worktree before it grades.
- `bench cache clean` stops every background grade and drops every queued grade first. It then takes its lock as it does today.
- A stopped grade writes no green, and its background record names the outcome `stopped`.
- The lifetime owner releases the resources of a stopped grade under ADR 0030.

`bench cache clean` stops background grades because a background grade is optional work.
