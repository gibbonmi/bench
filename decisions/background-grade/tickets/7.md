# What machine bound holds the background grades?

Blocked by: #2
Type: Grill

### Question

How many background grades run at once on one machine, at which width and priority?
What happens to a running or a queued grade when a newer tip supersedes its key?

### Answer

One background grade runs at a time on the machine, at `-p=2` and the lowest priority.
The reviewer chose this on 2026-10-27.

- At most one background grade runs at once on one machine. A second grade waits in the queue.
- A background grade runs its Go test commands at `-p=2` through a Bench-owned width input.
- The width input is not a member of the grade key. So a background green answers a consumer that runs at another width.
- A background grade runs at the lowest CPU and I/O priority that the platform allows.
- Grades deduplicate by grade key. A lane pass starts nothing when its key already has a fresh green, a running grade, or a queued grade.
- Each worktree keeps at most one queued grade.
- A newer lane-passed tip with a new key stops the running grade of its worktree and replaces the queued grade of its worktree. So the grade of a superseded tip takes no new test slot.
- The queue serves worktrees in the order of their lane passes.

This bound does not wait for the FT255 shared pool.
