# Where does the census measurement run, and what passes it?

Blocked by: #7
Type: Grill

### Question

ADR 24 requires a fresh resource census before adoption, and the outcome is admitted measure-first.
Does the measurement run now in shaping or as the first build ticket?
Which result passes, and which result stops the outcome?

### Answer

The first build ticket runs the census measurement before any code change, with a stop rule.
The reviewer chose this on 2026-10-27, and strengthened it after the map review.

- The measurement pins four cores to model a runner with two to four cores.
- The foreground workload is `bench test --package ./internal/gate` at the host configuration. The log records the width that it ran at.
- The ticket runs the foreground workload five times alone.
- The ticket then runs it five times beside one proxy of a whole gate.
- The proxy runs the Go test phases at `-p=2 -parallel=2` and at the lowest CPU and I/O priority.
- The log shows that the CPU priority and the I/O priority took effect on the host.
- The bound passes when two conditions hold. The median foreground wall time beside the proxy is at most 1.2 times the median alone. No foreground run goes red.
- If the bound does not pass, the build stops and reports the measurement to the reviewer.
- The ticket records each wall time, the two medians, the spread of the alone runs, and the host facts in the build log.

This measurement is the fresh resource census that ADR 0024 requires before adoption.
