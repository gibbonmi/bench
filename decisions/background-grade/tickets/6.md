# How does a consumer meet an in-flight background grade?

Blocked by: #1
Type: Grill

### Question

Today a lookup refuses while a live holder keeps the checkout lock or a pending cache.
Where does a background grade keep its state?
Does a consumer of the same grade key wait for it before it refuses or runs?

### Answer

A background grade claims its grade key, and a consumer of that key waits for it.
The reviewer chose this on 2026-10-27.

- A background grade holds a claim on its grade key while it runs. The claim names the key, the worktree, and the owner process.
- A background grade writes three places only: the shared evidence store, the delta record of its worktree, and the background record of its worktree.
- A background grade takes no checkout lock and never writes the gate cache of a checkout.
- Ticket 14 places the delta green, and ticket 9 places the background record.
- A consumer that finds no green looks for a running claim on its own key. If it finds one, it waits until the claim ends.
- After the wait, the consumer looks up the key again. A green answers it. Otherwise, the consumer runs its own gate.
- The gate timeout bounds the wait.
- A queued grade of the consumer's key leaves the queue when the consumer starts its own run. The consumer does not wait for a queued grade.
- Before a consumer runs its own gate, it stops each background grade of its worktree that claims another key.
- Two foreground runs in one checkout keep today's refusal: the run that cannot take the checkout lock refuses and names the owner.

This closes the second reviewer question of `roadmap/FT451.md` for background grades.
