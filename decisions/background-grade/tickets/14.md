# Where does a background delta green go?

Blocked by: #6, #8
Type: Grill

### Question

Ticket 8 gives the chunk subject the run decision of the chunk checkpoint, so a test-only change can run the delta set.
Ticket 6 lets a background grade write only the shared evidence store.
A delta green lives in the delta record of the worktree, and the store holds whole greens only.
Where does a background delta green go?

### Answer

A background delta green goes to the delta record of its worktree.
The reviewer chose this on 2026-10-27.

- A green background delta set writes the delta record of its worktree, as a foreground delta green does today.
- Only a later chunk checkpoint of the same base and subject in that worktree reads that record.
- A background whole green goes to the shared evidence store.
- Ticket 6 states the write rule that results from this answer.

A delta set costs a fraction of a whole gate, and FT445 built the delta record for this case.
