# What subject does a background grade compute?

Blocked by: #3, #5
Type: Grill

### Question

For each consumer in scope, which tree, schedule, and running executable does the background grade reproduce?
Which inputs may the subject read while the author keeps editing the worktree?

### Answer

A background grade computes the exact run of the next consumer of its worktree.
It reads only the graded commit and machine inputs.
The reviewer chose this on 2026-10-27.

- A kit worktree with an open spec build grades the chunk subject. That subject is the HEAD tree of the lane-passed tip without the review record of the spec.
- The chunk subject takes the run decision of the chunk checkpoint. So a test-only change after a whole green runs the delta set.
- The chunk subject runs under the running executable of the lane pass that started the grade.
- A change of that executable before the checkpoint moves the grade key, so the checkpoint then runs its own gate.
- Every other kit worktree grades the landing subject. That subject is the published tree of the tip, composed on the destination at the start of the grade.
- The landing subject runs under the installed broker and takes the schedule of the destination, as the complete checkpoint does.
- Every subject member reads the graded commit or machine inputs, and no member reads the live worktree.
- So an author edit, a plan commit, or a `bench probe` mutant during the run leaves the subject unchanged.

Ticket 14 decides where a background delta green goes.
