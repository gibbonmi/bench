# Who owns several light-path sources landed under one gate?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

The FT450 row states that FT314 owns the drain face of several light-path sources under one gate.
The draft spec leaves that face to the FT450 landing queue as a Won't handle.
The FT314 criteria do not name it.
Decide the owner.

### Answer

FT450 owns the landing of several light-path sources in order under one gate.
FT314 ships only the grade key and its lookup, which the FT450 queue reuses.
The next drain removes the FT450 row sentence that names FT314 as the owner.
