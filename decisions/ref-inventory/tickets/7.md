# How does the classifier pick the holder of a subsumed ref?

Blocked by: #1
Type: Grill

### Question

Of the 43 live refs, 21 share a tip with another ref, and 33 have a tip that another kept ref reaches.
A discard of every member of an equal-tip group loses the tip.
How does the classifier pick the holder, and what does the plan row show?

### Answer

Among refs with an equal tip, the lexically first ref is the root, and the others are subsumed by it.
A ref whose tip is a strict ancestor of another kept tip is subsumed by the ref that reaches it.
A holder is an active branch, a unique root, or a landed ref.
The plan row shows the class and the holder.
A subsumed row writes no discarded ref, because its commits stay reachable through the holder.
