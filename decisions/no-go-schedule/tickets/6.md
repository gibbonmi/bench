# How does a reduced green record, and what does it authorize?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

A full verdict and the green marker authorize later readers: the milestone
verification, the release preflight, and the reuse of a fresh green. The delta
record authorizes no landing. Which record does a reduced green write, does the
landing that it authorizes advance the green marker, and which readers accept
it?

### Answer

A green reader set writes a new record class, the **reader record**, with its own
reuse refusal. The landing publishes on a green reader record.

The landing does not advance the green marker. The marker stays at the tip of
the last whole green. The milestone verification, `bench prep-release`, the
reuse of a fresh green, and the FT314 reuse key do not accept a reader record.
