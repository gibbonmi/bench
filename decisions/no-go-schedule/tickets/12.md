# What does the ambient dashboard show for a tip that a reader record covers?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

After a reader-record landing, the gate cache of the primary checkout still
names the older tree. The gate signal then reads stale and routes to
`bench gate`, which runs the whole gate. What does the gate signal show for a
tip that a reader record covers, and which command does it route to?

### Answer

For a tip that a reader record covers, the gate signal shows
`reader green (<n> of 5)` and no command. `<n>` is the count of reader-record
landings since the green marker. The signal shows no stale row and no route to
`bench gate`. The next whole run comes from a landing under the bound of #8.
