# What happens when the whole run after reduced landings is red?

Blocked by: #8
Type: Grill

### Question

A census miss lets a reduced landing pass, and the next whole run reds on a
change it did not make. How does that red name the reduced landings since the
last whole green, and who repairs it?

### Answer

A whole run that is red after reader-record landings refuses its landing, like
any red. The refusal names each reader-record landing since the green marker.

The session then runs the whole gate on the destination alone. A red
destination is a confirmed defect, and it routes to `/bench-debug` before other
work. By the red-verdict case of #8, every later landing on that destination
runs the whole gate until a whole green moves the green marker.
