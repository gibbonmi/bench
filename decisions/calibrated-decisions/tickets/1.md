# What do Jev and the calibration papers establish?

Blocked by: none
Type: Research

### Question

Which claims in the TypeSafe Jev article rest on a published method? Which
implementation tactics in the calibration papers transfer to a process reward
in Bench?

### Answer

The article names "Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions" and
publishes no reward, no measure, and no procedure. Its evaluation grades
against an average of reference models, which is a preference label. Its
type-safety and zero-hallucination claims hold by schema construction for a
classifier and do not transfer to prose.

Four tactics transfer. RLCR gives a bounded proper scoring rule on a stated
confidence. Behavioral calibration scores an abstention zero and integrates
the risk threshold away. Rewarding Doubt gives a bounded integer confidence
scale, generated after the answer is frozen. Calibration-aware RL shows that
a binary reward alone keeps overconfidence. The full claim table, formulas,
contradictions, and unknowns are in
`decisions/calibrated-decisions/assets/calibration-research.md`.
