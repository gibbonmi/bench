# How is an abstention scored and counted?

Blocked by: #3
Type: Grill

### Question

When a delegate or a reviewer withholds a claim instead of stating one, what
score does the abstention receive, and what guards against over-abstention?

### Answer

An abstained claim carries the status `abstained` and no confidence. It
scores 0 and stays out of the Brier mean. The scorecard counts abstentions
as their own number, next to the claim count and the Brier mean. A rising
abstention count beside a falling claim count is then visible. No later probe
turns an abstention into a refuted claim.
