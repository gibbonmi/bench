# Is the first spec guidance-only, or guidance plus a scoring verb?

Blocked by: #6, #7
Type: Grill

### Question

The work splits at a clean boundary. One side is the guidance and scorecard
contract edits alone. The other side adds a Bench verb that computes the
score from structured records. Does the first spec stop at the guidance
boundary?

### Answer

The first spec stops at the guidance boundary. It edits `craft-line`,
`craft-delegate`, `craft-review`, the retro scaffold, and the scorecard
README. The retro author computes the Brier mean by hand from the retro
table. A scoring verb is out of this map until ten pairs exist for one
model, effort, and role.
