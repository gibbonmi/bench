# What evidence establishes progress?

Blocked by: #9
Type: Grill

### Question

Which evidence supports a progress label, and how are unsupported labels classified?

### Answer

A progress label requires a repaired requirement or defect with verification attached.
The agent may propose the label, but the experiment audits that label against its evidence.
Check outcomes alone, changed files, and unsupported author claims do not establish progress.
Unsupported labels remain unknown.
