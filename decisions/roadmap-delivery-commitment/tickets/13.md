# Which work classes take priority?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

How do defects and refactoring compare with new features?

### Answer

In general, defects and refactoring take priority over new features. Go modernization and the deepening refactors and fixes are the reviewer's current priority. A class label alone does not establish the work's purpose. This direction does not authorize automatic displacement of an existing commitment.
