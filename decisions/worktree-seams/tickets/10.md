# Which joins fields does the seam reduction keep?

Blocked by: #9
Type: Grill

### Question

Confirm the retained joins seam list that ticket 9 produces, field by field.
Decide each test that ticket 9 names as unable to convert.

### Answer

A top-tier consultation decided this ticket at the reviewer's direction.

The joins value keeps these 15 fields: `landReviewed`, `releaseLandingAssignment`,
`cleanupBoundary`, `cleanupLockAttempt`, `creationLockAttempt`,
`claimTakeoverGap`, `claimStealGap`, `restoreClean`, `chmodPool`,
`resolveRunningBinary`, `reauthorizeLock`, `reauthorizeBeforeCAS`, `resetLayers`,
`resetEnvelope`, and `buildSubject`. The other 12 fields go, and `now` and `home`
leave the joins value.

- Every uncontested verdict in the classification asset stands.
- `restoreClean` stays, because its test grades `claimAt` mid-cleanup. A hook
  fixture would grade a shell stand-in.
- The creation-lock interleaving stays injectable. Without the signal, a
  removed-lock mutation turns red only some of the time.
- `advanceLandingMarker` goes. Its fixture is a gate script in a real landing that
  deletes `refs/bench/green/<branch>` in the destination repository.
- `buildSubject` stays under rule (b). The build cancel test and the four
  sealed-broker rows stay as they are, and they do not stop the build.
- `resetEnvelope` stays, because a `reference-transaction` hook fixture needs a
  recursion guard.
- The spec writer may fold the two lock hooks into `Fault` steps. Each step is hit
  before its flock, and a nil fault stays a no-op. The creation step uses the
  `fault` parameter of `createAt`, and the cleanup step uses `cleanupBoundary`.
  The four tests that use the hooks keep their names.

Four GO fields depend on a build probe: `advanceLandingMarker`, `ignoredLstat`,
`pruneLandedBranches`, and `mergeReconcile`. If the probe for one of them fails,
that field stays with its tests unchanged. The build records the change for
reviewer veto and does not stop.
