# Which decisions does the inventory cover?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

The destination asks for every routine decision in the Bench workflow. Whose
decisions does the inventory cover?

### Answer

The inventory covers three families. The first family is the judgment calls
that an agent or a hook makes from prose rules. The second family is the
heuristics inside the Bench executable. The third family is the reviewer
decisions, as pre-answer candidates only. The inventory excludes each gate
check, because ticket #2 puts the gate verdict out of reach.
