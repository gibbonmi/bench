# Which authority ceilings hold for every candidate?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

Each candidate gets its own authority decision in ticket #10. Which limits
hold for every candidate, regardless of the inventory result?

### Answer

Two ceilings hold. A jev answer never changes or replaces a gate verdict. On
a reviewer decision, a jev answer is at most a pre-answer that the reviewer
confirms or rejects. Below the two ceilings, ticket #10 sets the authority of
each candidate: a hint, a route suggestion, or a binding agent call.
