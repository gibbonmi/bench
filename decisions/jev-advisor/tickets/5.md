# What evidence proves that a candidate fits jev?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

What evidence must a candidate show before the reviewer accepts that it fits
jev?

### Answer

A candidate needs two proofs in sequence. The paper screen comes first. It
accepts a candidate that reduces to atomic Choice, Score, or Noul questions on
bounded state. The candidate must also avoid each item on the jev-1.13
limitation list. A candidate that
passes then needs a replay probe against recorded Bench decisions before a
spec includes it. The paper screen alone is not sufficient.
