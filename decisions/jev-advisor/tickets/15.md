# How does local-jev enter the map?

Blocked by: none
Type: Grill

### Question

local-jev is a planned loopback server with the TypeSafe request shape. It
needs no key, and no content leaves the machine. Its plan states that it is
not behaviorally equivalent to jev. Its probabilities are not calibrated, and
it omits the `confidence` field. How does it enter the map?

### Answer

Ticket #1 stays as decided for the hosted jev. local-jev is the second arm of
the replay probe in ticket #9. Ticket #16 tracks whether local-jev runs, and
ticket #17 decides the endpoint after the probe. The rejected alternatives are
a local-only posture and a source note with no ticket.
