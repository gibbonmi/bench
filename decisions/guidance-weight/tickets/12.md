# Which part of each listed description does its trigger need?

Blocked by: none
Type: Research

### Question

A harness lists 26 kit skill and phase descriptions on every turn, and they held 6,188 bytes on 2026-10-11. For each description, what are its bytes, which phrases name its trigger, and which sentences repeat its skill or phase body?

### Answer

The inventory is `assets/description-inventory.md`, and the three reader pairs agree. Every listed description has content phrases that restate its body.

Four defects need a fix. Two phase descriptions have no trigger phrase, and the drain's phase label disagrees with its body. Two descriptions name a "threshold" that `.bench/BENCH.md` does not use. The invocation account in `.bench/BENCH-reference.md` disagrees with the policy table. A trim to the trigger and one leading-word phrase would remove about 1,700 bytes on every turn.
