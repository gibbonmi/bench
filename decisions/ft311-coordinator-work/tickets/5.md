# What does a generated delegate charge guarantee?

Blocked by: #1, #3
Type: Grill

### Question

Which charge fields are derived, and what happens when required evidence is missing or stale?

### Answer

A generated charge includes the assignment, frozen revision, approved ticket, ownership fence, required evidence, checks, and return format. Missing or stale required inputs prevent dispatch and identify the required repair. The coordinator supplies task-specific judgment. The generator invents no requirement.
