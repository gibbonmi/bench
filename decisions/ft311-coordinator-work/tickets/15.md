# Which measure decides whether delegation is cheaper?

Blocked by: #9
Type: Grill

### Question

How does the trial compare model cost and elapsed time, and what happens when cost data is missing?

### Answer

The trial reports total model cost and elapsed time separately. Model cost includes coordinator and delegate work, failed attempts, verification, and repairs. Missing usage or pricing data leaves cost unknown and prevents a cheaper claim. The reviewer decides whether an observed speed tradeoff is acceptable.
